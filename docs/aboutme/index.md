Howdy Yall My name is Ethan Cornett, and I’m a Mechanical Engineering major at UNC Charlotte. I chose engineering because I love the idea of creating things that can make people’s lives easier, more efficient, or simply better.
<img width="1096" height="1400" alt="IMG_3045" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a8e5c17-2327-4669-b45a-d3c3a047ab39" />

My interest in engineering started back in middle school when I joined VEX Robotics. What started as an activity quickly became something I genuinely enjoyed, and I’ve now been involved in robotics for 8 going on 9 years. Over that time, I’ve gone from simply enjoying building and competing with robots to developing a deeper interest in how things are designed, built, tested, and improved. Robotics was really my introduction to engineering, and it eventually showed me that this is what I want to do as a career.

As I’ve continued through college, I’ve been able to take that same curiosity from robotics and apply it to mechanical engineering. I enjoy the process of taking an idea, figuring out how to make it work, and then improving it when something doesn’t go as planned. That problem-solving process is one of the biggest reasons I enjoy engineering.

When I’m not working on engineering projects, I’m usually watching or playing sports, playing video games, or hanging out with friends. I also have a cat named Luna and a dog named Max, who are both a pretty big part of my life outside of school.

Looking toward the future, I’m interested in opportunities with companies such as Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, BMW, and Hendrick Motorsports or Hendrick Defense. Ultimately, I want to build a career where I can continue designing, building, and solving problems while working on things that have a real impact.

Take a look through my portfolio to see some of the projects I’ve worked on and how I’ve grown as an engineer.
