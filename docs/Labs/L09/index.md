# A9 – Lazer Cutter: 

## Objective 

In a group of 2-3 Design, laser cut, assemble, and document a parametric construction kit that accounts for laser cutter kerf. What you build is up to your team.
The kit must include: At least 3 unique part types. At least 8 total pieces. At least one press fit, slot fit, or tab fit connection. Evidence that you measured kerf and used it to set your fits and clearances. My group consisted of Morgan Gregory, Lexie Cox.
