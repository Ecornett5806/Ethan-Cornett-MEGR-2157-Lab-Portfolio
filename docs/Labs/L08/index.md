## Why does asking smart questions matter? 

As engineers, we ask questions to give us a better understanding of what we need to account for in our design and what nuances we need to plan around. Asking the right questions helps us identify potential problems before they happen and understand what the user actually needs. Without asking questions, we may overlook important details that could affect how well a product works. For example, when designing a bicycle light, we need to consider how visible it is at night, how easy it is to operate, and whether drivers can easily recognize the turn signals. By asking smart questions early in the design process, engineers can make better decisions, avoid unnecessary redesigns, and create products that better meet the needs of their users.

## Design Problem Statement:

Purpose of the Design:

The purpose of my design is to create a phone holder that props up a phone while allowing it to charge. The holder will include an opening for a charging cable so the phone can remain connected to its charger while sitting upright. The goal is to reduce the amount of desk space taken up by a phone and make it easier to keep the phone accessible while working.

How I Noticed the Problem:

I came up with this idea from my own experience doing homework at my desk. When my phone starts running low on battery, I normally have to place it flat on my desk while it charges. This takes up space on a desk that is already small, leaving me with less room for my laptop, notebooks, and other school supplies. I realized that a phone holder with an opening for a charging cable could solve this problem by keeping my phone upright while freeing up desk space.

Who Am I Designing For?

My ideal user is a student or anyone who regularly works at a desk and uses their phone throughout the day. These users may need to charge their phones while studying, working, or completing other tasks. The holder should be compact enough for a small desk, stable enough to support a phone, and designed to allow easy access to the charging port.

Design Approach:

My approach is to design a compact phone holder with a slanted support surface to keep the phone upright and an opening at the bottom to allow a charging cable to connect to the phone. I will focus on making the holder stable, minimizing the amount of desk space it occupies, and ensuring that the charging cable fits through the opening without being bent excessively. I will use CAD to create the design and evaluate its dimensions before 3D printing a prototype. After printing, I will test the holder with a phone to check its stability, fit, and ease of charging.

Research:

My research will focus on existing phone holders, the dimensions needed to accommodate a charging cable, and the design features that help a phone holder remain stable. I will compare existing designs to understand how their support angles, base sizes, and charging openings affect their usability. This information will help me determine the dimensions and features to include in my own design.
